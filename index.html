Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

AW139 Study Deck is provided by Richard Maas ("I", "me"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, contact me, or visit this privacy-policy website.

The app works offline, requires no account, and stores study progress on your device. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send your study answers or scratchpad notes to me.

Information stored by the app

To let you resume studying and view progress, the app stores information locally on your device:

  • Up to 365 completed study-session summaries, including session dates, topics, study mode, and correct, missed, or skipped totals.
  • Paused study sessions, including question order, selected answers, recall ratings, and scratchpad text. Newer versions keep separate sessions for each topic and study mode.

Weekly goals and activity milestones are calculated from this saved history. The app does not operate a cloud account or sync service, and I cannot access this local information. Device backups or transfers may include app data according to your operating system and backup settings.

Collection, tracking, and sharing

The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. It does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services. I do not sell or rent your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

TestFlight and Apple services

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash and usage information and shares it with me as the app provider. This may include device and operating-system details, app versions, session and crash information, and invitation details. Feedback you submit may include your comments, screenshots, and contact information.

I use this information to investigate problems and improve the app, and do not share TestFlight information with third parties. I retain any copies I make only as long as needed for those purposes. Apple controls retention within its own services. See Apple's TestFlight & Privacy notice and Apple's Privacy Policy for details of Apple's processing.

Contacting me

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. I use that information to respond and resolve your request. Email is handled by my email service provider, Gmail (Google). I retain correspondence only as long as needed for support and any applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in support messages.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, forms, or embedded third-party assets, and does not set cookies or use local storage. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's handling of website visitor information.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local study history remains until it is replaced by newer sessions or the app's data is removed. When upgrading to separate saved sessions, an older local progress record may be retained as a migration backup until you remove the app's data. On iOS, use Delete App to remove the app and its local data; Offload App keeps app data. If you use a browser version, clear that site's browser data. Backup copies are managed through your device or backup provider's settings.

You can contact me to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information I hold, including support correspondence and TestFlight information available to me. Rights may vary with your location. I cannot retrieve or erase study data held only on your device, or independently control information retained by Apple or GitHub.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy if the app or its privacy practices change. The current version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
